## Working interpretation

Informal collectors need a practical way to describe a lot, compare the net terms of a sale, reach a suitable receiving partner, agree what was received, and retain payment records. Limited connectivity, literacy barriers, uncertain prices and unreliable confirmations shape the proposed workflow.

These are project design assumptions and the team's interpretation. They are not a verbatim reproduction of the unavailable official brief or measured findings from a field study.

## Core scope

- Collector-side mobile interface with Hindi, Marathi and English text.
- Photo/voice-supported lot capture and a local draft queue.
- Manual category confirmation, sample offer comparison and transport deductions.
- Received quantity and final amount proposed by the recycler demo role and confirmed by the collector role.
- Separate payment claims, receipt confirmation, balances and downloadable records.

The [requirements matrix](requirements.md) maps these claims to code and verification. Requirements derived from the presentation must be rechecked against the issued official PS.

## Beyond this prototype

Real recipient verification, independent accounts, pickup scheduling, a buyer marketplace, trained models, live prices/maps, payment transfers, referral payouts, collector identity/loan integrations and downstream recycling proof are not completed capabilities. See [limitations](limitations.md).
