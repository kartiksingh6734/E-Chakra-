## Claims requiring new evidence

Official PS organisation/text, actual buyer eligibility, interviews/consent, measured income or diversion, willingness to pay, real-device success and trained-model performance remain unverified. Do not convert slide illustrations into measured results. For each future claim record the source, method, date, geography, sample size, denominator and known limits.
